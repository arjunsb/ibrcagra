IBRC has excelled in Various Biotechnology training programs in Molecular Biology, Recombinant DNA Technology (Genetic Engineering), Proteomics, Plant Tissue Culture, Basic Microbiology, Clinical Microbiology, Industrial Microbiology, Immunology, Biochemistry & Bioinformatics. It has also conducted various short-term training programs for UG and PG students and has accomplished long term projects for the partial fulfillment of degrees. Over a dozen of our students have been placed in various Companies and Industries. IBRC has also assisted Ph. D Work of various Ph. D. scholars. IBRC is also producing various biotechnological teaching kits and Buffers & Solution used in various practicals.

The Institute of Biotechnology and Research Centre (IBRC) is located at Deep Nagar, Sikandra, Agra. It was established in May, 2006 with a focus to nurture the talent and place its students in MNCs. In the first phase it was started with the aim to contribute the pivotal exchange of knowledge in the field of Biotechnology by providing the opportunity and encouraging the young and budding biotechnologists to learn about the practical techniques employed in various edges of Biotechnology such as, Molecular Biology, Immunology, Biochemistry, Bioinformatics and Microbiology. IBRC has achieved enormous success in it as the students of this institute get jobs at higher positions in various biotech Companies and research institutes. With the suggestion and demanding nature of multi-national Biotech companies, this institute is now running various PG, undergraduates degree and diploma programs.
